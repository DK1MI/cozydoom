How to play it

Load the WAD last, after any map packs:

gzdoom -iwad doom2.wad -file maps.wad cozydoom.wad
dsda-doom -iwad doom2.wad -file maps.wad cozydoom.wad
  • GZDoom / UZDoom use the ZSCRIPT and ZMAPINFO lumps, so replacements are exact.
  • dsda-doom, Woof, Nugget, PrBoom+, Eternity use the DEHACKED lump: each monster's slot takes over the replacement's stats, frames, sounds and flags (it keeps its own editor number and item drops).
  • Crispy / Chocolate Doom: add -dehlump. Replacing the Mancubus, Arachnotron, Baron, Cyberdemon or Spider Mastermind adds extra frames that Chocolate Doom does not support.
